Campus Facilities Knowledge Centre & Troubleshooting

Before filing a complaint, check these basic self-help tips for common facility issues.

1. Internet & Campus Wi-Fi Connection Issues

Symptoms: Wi-Fi connected but no internet, portal login page fails to load, or frequent disconnection.

  • Turn Wi-Fi off on your device, wait 10 seconds, and reconnect to CampusNet-5G.
  • Open your browser in incognito mode and enter http://1.1.1.1 or http://login.campus.internal to open the authentication portal.
  • Verify that your student network data quota has not expired.
  • If the entire floor access point is blinking red, submit an Internet/Wi-Fi ticket specifying your block and room number.

2. Electrical Sockets, Lights & Ceiling Fans

Symptoms: Tripped MCB breaker, switchboard sparking, fan regulator jammed, or tube light starter flicker.

  • Safety Warning: Do not insert metal wires or damp pins into damaged wall outlets.
  • Check if your high-power appliance (kettle, iron) caused the distribution board breaker to trip. Unplug it before resetting.
  • If a switchboard produces smoke or burning smell, flip off the main room switch and lodge a High Priority Electrical ticket immediately.

3. Water Supply, Tap Dripping & Restroom Drains

Symptoms: Leaking washbasin tap, flush cistern continuous overflow, or clogged floor drain.

  • Locate the shut-off angle valve under the washbasin and rotate clockwise to halt heavy overflow.
  • Never dispose of paper napkins, plastics, or hair down shower drains.
  • Submit a Plumbing ticket indicating the wing, floor, and cubicle number.

4. Projectors, HDMI Cables & Podium Audio

Symptoms: "No Source Signal" on overhead projector, audio feedback whistle, or projector lamp warning light.

  • On Windows, press Win + P and select Duplicate or Extend display mode.
  • Check if the switcher on the podium is toggled to Input 1 (VGA) or Input 2 (HDMI).
  • For wireless microphones, verify the receiver is powered ON and replace AA batteries in the transmitter.
  • Lodge a Classroom Equipment ticket mentioning the specific classroom or lecture theater.

5. Broken Benches, Chairs & Cupboards

Symptoms: Wobbly desk legs, broken armrest, missing screws, or jammed wardrobe door hinges.

  • Move unstable chairs away from aisles to prevent accidental falls.
  • Submit a Furniture complaint with a clear photograph so the carpenter can bring the appropriate brackets and screws.

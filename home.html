Welcome to CampusFix

CampusFix is a centralized digital complaint and maintenance management portal for college students, faculty, and administrative staff. Report campus infrastructure issues, track repairs in real-time, and ensure timely resolution across hostels, laboratories, and classrooms.

The Problem We Solve

Traditional manual complaint handling in college institutions has multiple drawbacks:

  • Complaints written in physical registers get delayed or overlooked.
  • Students cannot check the status of their repair requests.
  • Lack of technician accountability and resolution timelines.
  • No structured mechanism to record student feedback post-repair.
Proposed Digital Solution

CampusFix streamlines campus facility maintenance with a modern digital pipeline:

  • Unique tracking ID generated for every registered complaint.
  • Categorized dispatch for electrical, plumbing, network, and equipment.
  • Transparent 3-stage progress: Submitted → In Progress → Resolved.
  • 5-Star rating system to maintain quality campus standards.
How It Works
Step 1: Submit

Student lodges a ticket with location, description, category, and optional photo.

Step 2: Assign

Admin reviews ticket, sets priority level, and assigns maintenance personnel.

Step 3: Repair

Technician resolves the issue and updates status to Resolved with remarks.

Step 4: Feedback

Student reviews the completed repair and provides a 1-to-5 star rating.

Emergency or Hazardous Issues?

For direct electrical sparking, smoke, or heavy pipeline breaks, please inform your hostel warden or floor supervisor immediately in addition to filing a ticket online.
